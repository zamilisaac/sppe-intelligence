# Source data

Source: Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM), OpenDOSM — https://open.dosm.gov.my. Please cite "Source: Department of Statistics Malaysia" when reusing.

Duplicate uploads from the project were removed (28 uploads = 16 distinct files, checked by checksum).

| File (`dosm/`) | Content | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `productivity_annual.csv` | National GDP, hours, employment, productivity, 22 sectors/sub-sectors | 2015–2025 |
| `productivity_annual_p14.csv` | National productivity, 14 sub-sectors coded special.01–14 (no names in file) | 2015–2025 |
| `gdp_state_real_supply.csv` | Real GDP (constant 2015 prices) and YoY growth by state × sector | 2015–2025 |
| `wages_state.csv` | Survey median/mean wages by state × sex | 2010–2025 |
| `wages_sector.csv` | Survey wages, national, 5 sectors + overall | 2010–2025 |
| `wages_industry.csv` | Survey wages, national, MSIC sections A–S | 2010–2025 |
| `wages_education.csv` | Survey wages by education, national | 2010–2025 |
| `wages_skill.csv` | Survey wages by skill, national | 2010–2025 |
| `formal_wages_state.csv` | Formal-sector median wage and employees by state | Monthly, Jan 2022–Jun 2026 |
| `formal_wages_msic.csv` | Formal-sector wages, national, by sector × industry | Monthly, Jan 2022–Jun 2026 |
| `formal_wages_band.csv` | Formal employees by wage band, national | Monthly, Jan 2022–Jun 2026 |
| `lfs_state_sex.csv` | Labour force, employed, unemployed, rates by state × sex (no Malaysia row) | 1982–2025 |
| `cpi_2d_state.csv` | CPI index by state × 13 divisions + overall (no Malaysia row) | Monthly, Jan 2010–Sep 2026 |
| `cpi_2d_state_inflation.csv` | Inflation by state × division | Monthly, Jan 2010–Sep 2026 |
| `hies_state.csv` | Household income, expenditure, Gini, poverty by state | 2022, 2024 |
| `hies_state_percentile.csv` | Household income by percentile | 2019, 2022, 2024 |

Not included: the DOSM *Labour Market Review Q3 2025* PDF used as a cross-check. Its terms do not allow redistribution, so get it from https://www.dosm.gov.my.
